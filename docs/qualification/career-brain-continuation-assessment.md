# Career Brain qualification continuation

## Qualification verdict

**DO NOT PROMOTE.** The remaining native checks are complete. Identity and sampled unsupported-answer/admission safety hold, but whole-assertion grounding and factual-state consistency still fail. No production code, database migration or deployment was promoted.

The smallest blocking failure is concrete: a packet correctly supports SQL work while its complete canonical bullet claims an implemented warning that the source describes only as a preference. The default Luna Pro audits still accept the longer historical paragraph. Actual ResumeIR compilation reproduces that unsupported assertion from the unchanged retained proposal. This is a deterministic diagnostic of an unaccepted synthetic proposal, not a published owner résumé or a newly observed public answer.

The prior [assessment](career-brain-repair-assessment.md), benchmark revisions, failed runs, raw proposals and accounting remain preserved. The [continuation manifest](../../experiments/career-brain/v2/continuation/results/manifest.json) freezes 38 earlier artifacts and a separate input hash. The original $12 ledger remains exhausted; this work uses a separate $5 ceiling.

## Final architecture selected

**No new architecture is qualified for promotion.** Retain the current production architecture. The preferred experimental candidate remains full-source Luna Pro extraction → application-owned identity/alias reconciliation → exact multi-span provenance → bounded factual audit and conditional omitted-entity coverage → factual equivalence/diff → explicit owner review and atomic apply. Semantic/lexical retrieval supplies candidates; bounded packets carry canonical interpretation, exact source language, relationships, ownership and uncertainty; typed support adjudication controls answers and résumé admission.

A mandatory document map is not justified by the preserved ablation. A targeted Sol audit caught the unchanged historical warning overstatement in this continuation, but one paired record does not qualify universal Sol escalation. The selectable-assertion audit passed minimal contrasts yet missed that same longer paragraph, so it is not promoted as a certified repair. No provider-side subagents were used.

Selection is driven by trustworthy output, not call count or latency. Whole rendered assertions need source-grounded admission after equivalence; pending conflicts need a clear serving/publication policy. Adding stages without fixing those boundaries does not qualify the architecture.

## Answer faithfulness and false abstention

Thirty fresh Luna answers received only their own question's compact packets. Direct inspection and independent bounded audits found **0/15 unsupported affirmative qualification answers**, **0/13 outright supported-question false abstentions**, and **0/2 partial answers inflated into full qualifications**. These small controlled samples are not a population safety guarantee.

All **30/30** answers returned valid supplied citation IDs. The independent audit flagged one course answer for omitting the explicitly fictional credential qualifier; this conservative finding is retained. Nine answers assumed she/her pronouns not supplied by the first-person source, and one leadership answer addressed the candidate as “you.” Citation-ID validity does not establish complete source/presentation faithfulness.

The automation answer now describes the actual work and measurement but still hedges on the concept. The SQL answer acknowledges the implemented checks while withholding unstored details of what they tested. The zero-abstention metric counts complete refusals of known facts, not perfect completeness or perfect affirmative phrasing.

## Résumé evidence admission

Actual ResumeIR checks produced **0/20 unsupported admissions** on the cached negative queries and **0/17** on the curated non-direct probes. No compiled evidence ID escaped its admitted set. Both genuine partial probes were excluded from automatic admission. Conservative classification errors also omitted two directly supported curated cases.

These clean-gold results do not qualify the complete ingest-to-résumé path. The controlled malformed-canonical canary and replay of the exact retained R4 paragraph both admit an unsupported warning assertion alongside valid SQL evidence. Packet support for a requested proposition cannot certify every sentence of a whole canonical bullet.

## Cost and latency

The continuation used **77 OpenRouter attempts**: 46 Luna Pro, 30 Luna and one Sol. All 77 have native usage events with reported cost. Known reported subtotal: **$0.196778**; consumed conservative reservations: **$4.73/$5**. No failed reservation was refunded, no old ledger was reset, and no public quota changed.

Native tokens were 865,855 input / 233,481 output for Luna Pro; 14,842 / 3,475 for Luna; 2,646 / 363 for Sol. Reported cost is not a provider invoice guarantee.

Including the preserved run: **242 total attempts**, **$0.948904 known subtotal**, **17 historical unknown-cost attempts/events**, and **$16.18 consumed reservations across the separate $12 + $5 ceilings**. No new Cohere batches were needed; the existing embedding model and indexed candidates were retained.

Rich-repeat median latency was **101.1 seconds (1.7 minutes)**. V2/V3 native flows took **125.9 / 164.7 seconds (2.1 / 2.7 minutes)**. The earlier 145-project rich boundary remains 6.4 minutes. Multi-minute creation latency is acceptable; the grounding and consistency defects determine the verdict.
