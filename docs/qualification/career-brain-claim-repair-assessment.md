# Career Brain claim admission and grounding qualification

**DO NOT PROMOTE.** Claim-level admission and whole-bullet verification repair the
observed résumé leak, and persisted conflicts now block stale attendance. Factual
diff qualification still fails: two of five identical-source imports propose
removing approved relationships without changed evidence. Owner review remains
necessary, but these removals must be classified using source evidence before
they become actionable updates.

Continue from draft PR #1 / `03650f2`. No document-map, chunking, ranking, vector,
billing or product redesign was undertaken. All **144** artifacts from the v2 and
previous continuation result namespaces and benchmark are hash-protected by the
new [manifest](../../experiments/career-brain/v2/claim-repair/results/manifest.json).

## Selected architecture

Retain full-source Luna Pro rich extraction, bounded record audit, conditional
coverage and application identity. Add bounded actor equivalence, approved claim
availability, compact exact-span packets, individual source-grounded claim
admission, Luna bullet composition, independent Luna Pro whole-bullet verification
and at most one single-admitted-claim simplification plus recheck. Unknown or
incomplete verification fails closed. Canonical summaries and linked skill lists
are not admitted as factual shortcuts.

Sol was tested only on thirteen Pro FAIL/REVIEW cases. It agreed on all thirteen,
rescued no supported case and provided no measured improvement here. There is no
evidence for Sol on every bullet or a more complicated ingest architecture.
